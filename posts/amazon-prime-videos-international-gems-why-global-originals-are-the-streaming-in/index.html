Amazon Prime Video's International Gems: Why Global Originals Are the Streaming Industry's Best-Kept Secret

2026-07-10 · Amazon Prime · Tags: amazon prime, Amazon, streamingservicepro

When most cord-cutters think about the streaming wars, the same three names dominate the conversation: Netflix, Disney+, and HBO Max. Amazon Prime Video, despite being one of the largest platforms in the world, often gets relegated to a footnote—a convenient add-on to a Prime membership rather than a destination in its own right. But that perception is changing, and the shift is coming from an unexpected corner: international originals. While Netflix has poured billions into global productions and Disney+ leans heavily on its American franchises, Amazon Prime Video has quietly built a library of foreign-language series that rival—and sometimes surpass—the best of what its competitors offer. From Indian crime thrillers to Spanish historical epics and Korean dramas, these hidden gems are not just filler; they are the future of streaming. In this deep dive, we'll explore why Amazon's global strategy is a game-changer, how it compares to the other big players, and why your next binge-worthy show might be in a language you've never heard spoken.

The Global Advantage: Amazon's Quiet International Expansion

Amazon Prime Video's approach to international content is fundamentally different from that of its rivals. Netflix, for instance, has made a massive splash with hits like Money Heist and Squid Game, but its strategy often feels like a scattergun—throwing money at every market in hopes of finding the next viral sensation. Disney+ has largely stuck to its established franchises, with only a few international productions like Lupin (French) and The Mandalorian (which is American but has global appeal). HBO Max, meanwhile, has focused on prestige English-language content, with only occasional forays into foreign series like My Brilliant Friend (Italian).

Amazon, on the other hand, has been methodically building a global library that feels curated and intentional. The company has invested heavily in India, where it produces more original series than any other streaming service. Shows like Mirzapur, The Family Man, and Paatal Lok have become cultural phenomena, not just in India but among international audiences who appreciate their gritty storytelling and complex characters. Similarly, Amazon has partnered with Spanish production houses to create epic dramas like El Cid and La Templanza, and it has delved into the booming Korean market with titles like My Name and Island. This isn't just about filling a quota; it's about recognizing that great stories transcend borders.

What sets Amazon apart is its willingness to let these shows breathe. Unlike Netflix, which often cancels international series after one or two seasons if they don't achieve global breakout status, Amazon tends to give its originals more time to find an audience. This patient approach has paid off, as several of its international shows have developed cult followings that rival any English-language hit. For cord-cutters, this means a treasure trove of content that you won't find on other platforms—unless you're willing to pay for multiple subscriptions.

Beyond the Mainstream: Hidden Features That Enhance the Experience

While the content itself is a major draw, Amazon Prime Video also offers a suite of features that make it a superior choice for international viewing. The most notable is X-Ray, a tool that lets you see the cast, crew, and trivia for any scene in real time. For foreign-language shows, X-Ray becomes invaluable—it can display the names of actors, provide context about cultural references, and even show you the original language title. This feature is something that Netflix, Disney+, and HBO Max simply don't offer at the same level. Netflix has a similar feature called "Secrets" for some shows, but it's not as comprehensive or as seamlessly integrated.

Another underrated feature is Amazon's support for multiple audio tracks and subtitles. While all major platforms offer subtitles, Amazon's implementation is particularly robust. You can often choose from dozens of subtitle languages, and the platform automatically syncs them with the audio track. This is a godsend for international content, where you might want to watch with the original audio and English subtitles, or even switch to a dubbed version if you prefer. Amazon also allows you to adjust subtitle size and style, which is a small but significant customization that many viewers appreciate.

For cord-cutters, Amazon Prime Video also integrates seamlessly with other Amazon services. If you have a Fire TV Stick, you can use Alexa to search for shows across multiple apps, including Netflix and Hulu, but Prime Video's own content is always front and center. The platform also supports watch parties, allowing you to sync your viewing with friends and family, which is perfect for discovering international gems together. These features might seem minor, but they add up to a more polished and user-friendly experience than what you'll find on many competitors.

Original Series That Deserve Your Attention: A Curated List

If you're ready to dive into Amazon Prime Video's international offerings, here are a few shows that should be at the top of your list. These aren't just good for foreign-language series; they're good, period, and they hold their own against anything on Netflix or HBO Max.

  • The Family Man (India) – A gripping espionage thriller that follows a middle-class man who secretly works for a counter-terrorism unit. It's a perfect blend of action, drama, and social commentary, and it's one of the best shows Amazon has ever produced.
  • Mirzapur (India) – Set in the lawless city of Mirzapur, this crime saga is often compared to Breaking Bad for its intense storytelling and morally ambiguous characters. It's raw, violent, and utterly addictive.
  • El Cid (Spain) – A historical epic that reimagines the life of the legendary Spanish knight. The production values are stunning, and the performances are top-notch. It's a visual feast that transports you to medieval Spain.
  • My Name (South Korea) – A revenge thriller that follows a woman who joins a drug cartel to find her father's killer. It's fast-paced, brutal, and features some of the best action sequences in any streaming series.
  • La Templanza (Spain) – Based on the novel by María Dueñas, this period drama follows a wine merchant in 19th-century Mexico and Spain. It's a lush, romantic story that showcases Amazon's ability to produce high-quality literary adaptations.

These shows are just the tip of the iceberg. Amazon also has excellent French series like Le Bureau des Légendes (The Bureau), which is often cited as one of the best spy dramas ever made, and German productions like Deutschland 83 that offer a fresh perspective on the Cold War. The point is, if you're only watching English-language content on Prime Video, you're missing out on a whole world of storytelling.

The Value Proposition: How Prime Video Stacks Up Against the Competition

When comparing streaming services, price is always a factor. Netflix's standard plan costs around $15.49 per month, Disney+ is $13.99, and HBO Max (now just Max) is $15.99. Amazon Prime Video, on the other hand, is included with a Prime membership, which costs $14.99 per month or $139 per year. But here's the kicker: that membership also includes free shipping on Amazon orders, access to Prime Music, Prime Reading, and a host of other benefits. So if you're already an Amazon shopper, Prime Video is essentially free.

Even if you don't use the shipping benefits, Prime Video's standalone subscription is just $8.99 per month, making it the cheapest of the major services. And what do you get for that price? A library that includes not only the international gems we've discussed but also a robust selection of licensed movies and TV shows, plus a growing slate of original series like The Boys, Fleabag, and The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel. When you factor in the ability to add on premium channels like HBO Max, Showtime, and Starz à la carte, Prime Video becomes a powerful hub for cord-cutters who want to customize their viewing experience without paying for a bloated cable bundle.

Compare that to Netflix, which has been raising prices while canceling fan-favorite shows, or Disney+, which is increasingly focused on its own franchises and less on diverse international content. HBO Max has the prestige factor, but it's also the most expensive and doesn't offer the same breadth of global programming. For the value-conscious streamer, Amazon Prime Video is the clear winner—especially when you consider the hidden gems that other platforms simply don't have.

Cord-Cutting Strategies: Making Prime Video Your Streaming Hub

If you're serious about cutting the cord, Amazon Prime Video should be the centerpiece of your setup. Here's why: it's the only major service that allows you to subscribe to other streaming platforms directly through its interface. You can add HBO Max, Showtime, Paramount+, and even niche services like BritBox or Acorn TV as channels, and you'll get a single bill and a unified interface. This is a game-changer for cord-cutters who want to avoid the hassle of managing multiple apps and subscriptions.

Moreover, Prime Video's integration with Fire TV devices means you can use voice commands to search across all your streaming apps, not just Prime Video. You can say, "Alexa, find Succession," and it will show you where to watch it, whether it's on HBO Max (which you've added as a channel) or on a different service. This level of convenience is unmatched by Netflix, Disney+, or HBO Max, which are all walled gardens that only show their own content.

For those who want to maximize their savings, consider this strategy: subscribe to Prime Video as your base, then add one or two premium channels based on your viewing habits. For example, if you love HBO's prestige dramas, add HBO Max for a few months, binge what you want, then cancel and switch to Showtime for a while. This à la carte approach is the essence of cord-cutting, and Amazon makes it easier than any other platform. Plus, with the international content we've highlighted, you'll never run out of things to watch, even during the months when you're not paying for an add-on.
